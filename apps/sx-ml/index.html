UMAP explorer

A million SPHEREx spectra (TARS table 2) as points in a UMAP embedding of their normalized spectral shapes, or of their residuals from the empirical single-star template. Colour by any catalog, fit or embedding column; hover and click for a star's spectrum and fits; shift+drag to lasso a group and see its median residual spectrum against the single-star templates. Links go to the sx-viz star page.

Spectral sequence

The empirical one-parameter template library: drag the slider along the sequence (by arc length, parameter or mapped Teff) and watch the median SPHEREx spectrum change, with its population scatter, the model atmosphere at the same Teff, the second empirical parameter (first residual eigenvector) and example stars.
