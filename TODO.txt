  [ ] SDF/NDF transit + CPV crossmatch (2026-11-10, first pass in
      results/deepfield_xmatch_20261110/): 35 hot-Jupiter TOIs (P<5 d,
      Rp>7 Re) with >1000 images; 18 not in any bulk run -> run a small
      bulk list with --psf.  Per-point scatter is 4-5x flux_err_jy for
      T~10-11 hosts: test whether PSF photometry closes the gap.
      PSF run done 2026-11-11 (3401 TARS P<1 d rotators added):
      /ar0/SPHEREX/bulkphot/deepfield_psf_v0.  PSF does not reduce the
      per-point scatter (PSF/aperture 1.06; bands 5-6 0.93-0.97).  The
      excess over flux_err_jy (3-5x in bands 1-4) is white per exposure,
      not spectral-model error (same scatter at equal wavelength), weakly
      intrapixel (+-3-10 ppt), and not a per-image zero point (no
      correlation with other stars on the same image).
      2026-11-11: the term is a fixed detector pattern (delta-flat).
      Residuals of different stars within 0.3 px on the same detector
      correlate at 0.45-0.5 (same qr; 0.28 across qr2/qr3), independent
      of flux ratio and stable over 300 d.  A (det, qr) map solved from
      518M residuals of T<12.5 tars4_v0 stars (deep-field stars held
      out) cuts bright-star scatter to 0.63-0.79x in bands 1-5 (qr2)
      and transit-depth errors by 18%.  Maps and scripts are in
      results/deepfield_xmatch_20261110/noise/.  Neighbour (CPM-like)
      regression and regression on the star's own pixels do not help
      (same-exposure correlation at 20-160 px is 0.04).
